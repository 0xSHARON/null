HOW TO PLAY NULL

STANDARD UNO PROTOCOL WITH A SYSTEM PURGE TWIST.

01. THE OBJECTIVE

Deallocate your entire hand before your opponents. Match the active discard pile by color or digit. Dump pointers until your register reaches zero.

02. ACTION CARDS

Deploy SKIP to suppress turns, REVERSE to invert direction, and +2 to flood opponent registers with penalty draws.

03. WILD MODIFIERS

Deploy WILD to reassign active color. Unleash WILD +4 to force draw penalties while hijacking the color cycle.

04. THE SIGNATURE 'NULL' CARD

A fatal deallocation event. Can be played anytime to wipe the active penalty buffer clean and reset the entire stack to neutral.

05. CALLING NULL!

When you have only 1 card remaining in your hand, you MUST hit CALL NULL! before ending your turn. If caught, suffer a +2 card penalty!

06. VICTORY CONDITIONS

The first player to play their final card triggers a SYSTEM PURGE and wins the round. Opponents tally score points based on card weight.
